About Our SMS Program

Mike's Auto Shop, located at 1234 Main St, Dallas TX 75201, operates an SMS/text messaging program to communicate with customers and contacts who have explicitly opted in to receive messages from us.

Our messaging program keeps you informed about your Auto Repair needs — from appointment confirmations to helpful follow-ups and occasional offers.

Types of Messages We Send

  • Appointment reminders and booking confirmations
  • Follow-up messages after inquiries or service visits
  • Customer care and support communications
  • Promotional offers and special announcements
  • General business updates relevant to your account
Message & Data Rates

Standard message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and plan. Mike's Auto Shop is not responsible for any charges imposed by your wireless carrier. Message frequency varies based on your activity and our communications schedule.

Age Requirement

Our SMS program is intended for individuals who are 18 years of age or older. By opting in, you confirm that you meet this age requirement.

Section 03

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Mikes Auto Shop collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you interact with our website, contact forms, and SMS messaging program.

Information We Collect

  • Contact information — name, email address, and mobile phone number
  • Inquiry details — the nature of your service request or question
  • Communication records — history of messages exchanged with our business
  • Opt-in/opt-out records — timestamps and method of your SMS consent or withdrawal
  • Usage data — browser type, IP address, and pages visited on our website

How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver the SMS messages you opted in to receive
  • To respond to your service inquiries and requests
  • To send appointment reminders and follow-up communications
  • To improve the quality of our communications and services
  • To comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations

SMS Opt-In Details

We collect your phone number and SMS consent through our contact form on this page. Consent is obtained via two separate unchecked, optional checkboxes — one for transactional messages and one for promotional messages. Neither checkbox is pre-checked. Transactional and promotional consent are never combined. We maintain records of opt-in timestamps and consent language shown at the time of sign-up. You may withdraw consent at any time by replying STOP.

Cookies & Tracking

Our website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience and analyze site traffic. You may disable cookies through your browser settings. We do not use tracking data to send targeted SMS messages.

Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Your Rights

  • Unsubscribe from SMS at any time by replying STOP
  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion by contacting us at mike@mikesautoshop.com
⚠ Critical — No Sharing of SMS Opt-In Data

Mikes Auto Shop will never sell, rent, lease, share, or disclose your mobile phone number or SMS opt-in information to any third party for their own marketing or promotional purposes. Your phone number is used solely to send messages on behalf of Mikes Auto Shop. This data is not shared with affiliates, lead brokers, or any outside organizations for marketing use.

Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted technology providers solely to deliver communications on our behalf. These providers are contractually bound to protect your information and prohibited from using it for any purpose other than providing services to Mikes Auto Shop. We do not purchase, rent, or use any third-party lead lists for our SMS program.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services or as required by law. You may request deletion at any time by emailing mike@mikesautoshop.com.

Section 04

Terms of Service

These Terms of Service govern your participation in the SMS messaging program operated by Mikes Auto Shop. By opting in, you agree to these terms.

SMS Program Description

Mikes Auto Shop operates an SMS messaging program to communicate with opted-in contacts regarding Auto Repair services. We send two separate categories of messages, each requiring independent consent:

  • Transactional messages — appointment confirmations, appointment reminders, missed call follow-ups, and customer care communications
  • Promotional messages — special offers, win-back campaigns, service reminders, and marketing communications

Message frequency varies based on your engagement with our business. Consent for transactional messages is never combined with consent for promotional messages.

How to Opt Out

  • Reply STOP to any message — you will be immediately unsubscribed
  • Reply UNSUBSCRIBE or CANCEL to any message
  • Email us at mike@mikesautoshop.com and request removal
  • Call us at +1 469-966-2295 during business hours

After opting out, you will receive one final confirmation text. No further messages will be sent unless you choose to opt back in.

How to Get Help
